# Kata

Kata helps people arrange supplied content so its intended message remains clear. Work with the content as provided: consider its placement, available space, grouping, and reading order. Do not write or rewrite prose, derive relationships, redesign the inside of a supplied chart, table, infographic, code sample, or other content, or invent material to make a layout fit.

The brief and supplied content may state an audience, question, or priority. Use these to assess layout. Do not invent a primary claim or stronger priority. If a consequential layout choice depends on missing intent, ask; otherwise preserve the supplied treatment or give equally important material equal emphasis.

## Choose or retain a form

Supplied content does not need to acquire a Kata pattern to be a valid input. A concise result such as “keep the current layout; it preserves the stated emphasis and remains readable” is complete when no change is needed.

The references describe six existing forms and retain their worked specimens. Those specimens illustrate the catalogue's history and affordances; some demonstrate organizing or authoring information beyond Kata's current supplied-content scope. Do not treat them as authorization to create headlines, classify content, derive relationships, or rewrite source material. Use a form only to arrange supplied material whose relevant relationship and priority are already stated. If the source does not support the relationship, do not infer it.

| Existing form | Supplied relationship or priority | Read |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aligned comparison | Alternatives are already presented on common criteria | [comparison](references/comparison.md) |
| Whole and parts | Parts and the whole are already identified | [composition](references/composition.md) |
| Nested groups | Membership is already specified | [hierarchy](references/hierarchy.md) |
| Directed dependencies | The entities and edge meaning are already specified | [relationship](references/relationship.md) |
| Time series | A consistently defined measure is supplied at ordered times | [change over time](references/change-over-time.md) |
| Asymmetric composition | The brief supports one item receiving more space beside its context | [golden cut](references/golden-cut.md) |

Read [shared layout assessment](references/layout-assessment.md) for checks that apply across forms, including when to keep the current arrangement. Then read the relevant form reference for its particular constraints. If none fits, explain the layout constraint and what supplied information or intent would change the recommendation. Do not silently shorten text, remove facts, or redesign the content. Preserve any association between content and its caption, source, unit, or qualification.

## Responsibility and output

Kata's current responsibility is the arrangement of supplied content: placement, available space, grouping, and reading order. It does not own content authorship or internal information modeling. The renderer owns output behavior and format; an optional brand skill owns visual identity. See [integration](references/integration.md). For Lys, use the available Lys authoring guidance. Kata works without Lys or a brand skill and does not install either.

Only claim an integration was tested when the actual output has been inspected. Review the rendered result in its intended contexts; structural validity alone does not establish readability. Keep authoring instructions out of the audience-facing content.

The worked examples use fictional data and are specimens, not evidence to reuse in a real presentation. The rationales are design judgments; Gestalt vocabulary supplies analogies, not proof of universal effectiveness. Support specific empirical psychology claims with relevant research and describe its limits.
