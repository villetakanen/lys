NPCs declare first, then characters, then bosses. Actions resolve simultaneously; in a contest, the highest individual die goes first.

THE PRICE OF SURVIVAL

What will you sacrifice?

After armor and any Attribute spend, resolve each remaining level of Harm separately.

Take the hit

Fill an empty Harm slot with Transient Harm.

Each filled slot costs one die. Transient Harm clears during downtime.

Bear the scar

Make an existing Transient Harm Permanent.

Match the Harm type when typed. The slot stays filled and the consequence lasts.

Break the gear

Break gear bound to the Attribute corresponding to the Harm.

The item stops working. Its Binding cost remains.

If none of these options can resolve the Harm, you are Ousted. Your character’s story ends.
